AMVerge Extension

Scene Detection & Direct Layer Import

AMVerge gives you a faster, visual way to skim through video files or active comp layers and import clean scenes directly into After Effects.

Benefits

  • Visual Grid Browsing: Skim scenes instantly with live hover video preview.
  • Direct AE Layering: Import selected clips sequentially into your active composition with automatic scaling.
  • Smart Scene Detection: Fast keyframe-aligned cuts or AI TransNetV2 deep learning boundary detection.
  • App Cache Sync: Access your desktop AMVerge cache episodes directly inside AE.

Selecting & Importing

  • Single Click: Select clip and load into preview player.
  • Shift + Click: Range-select between the last clicked clip and current clip.
  • Import Button: Adds all selected scenes to your AE composition as layered footage items.
